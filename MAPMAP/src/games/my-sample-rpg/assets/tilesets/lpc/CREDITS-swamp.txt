## Swamp (chapter 2)

Dead trees in the sunken forest (swamp_deadtree_* tiles in town-32.png, cut by scripts/append-swamp-tiles.py):
"[LPC] Trees" by bluecarrot16 and the authors listed in CREDITS-trees.txt. CC-BY-SA 3.0.
https://opengameart.org/content/lpc-trees

Rune stele (swamp_stele_* tiles): restored by mirroring the intact half of the LPC cavern & ruins stele
(see CREDITS-cavern-ruins.txt). CC-BY-SA 3.0.

Swamp grass, mud, swamp tree and bank tiles are recolors/derivatives of this project's town-32 tiles.

Boardwalk decks and the ferry raft (swamp_deck_*, swamp_raft tiles):
"LPC style wood bridges and steel flooring" by Xenodora. CC-BY-SA 3.0 / GPL 3.0 / GPL 2.0.
https://opengameart.org/content/lpc-style-wood-bridges-and-steel-flooring

Reed village thatched houses (swamp_house_* tiles) are recolors of this project's town-32 cottage tiles.
Chapter 2 NPC looks (id:miren, id:odi, id:tobin, id:reed_guard) are clothing recolors of the existing
LPC NPC sheets (see assets/characters/lpc/CREDITS.csv), made by scripts/add-npc-variants.py.
